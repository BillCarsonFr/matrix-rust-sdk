Added `Room::sendDelayedEvent`, `Room::sendDelayedStateEvent` and `Room::updateDelayedEvent` (with the new `DelayedEventUpdateAction` enum) for delayed events ([MSC4140](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4140)), plus `Client::sendToDeviceMessage` and `Client::subscribeToToDeviceMessages` for sending and receiving custom to-device messages, optionally Olm-encrypted. Together these make it possible to drive MatrixRTC natively, without going through a widget.
